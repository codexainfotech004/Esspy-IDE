🧠 1. Arduino Uno Controller

The main microcontroller board. Digital pins D2 and D3 connect to the IR Sensors. Motor driver pins connect to D9, D8 and D10, D12.

📡 2. Dual IR Sensor Modules

Mounted facing downwards (1-2 cm above ground). Detects reflection contrast between black tape and white floor.

⚙️ 3. BO Motors & Motor Driver

Geared DC motors on rear left & right. Caster wheel on front provides 360-degree smooth pivoting motion.

🔋 4. Battery Power Pack

Connects 7.4V/9V battery to controller & motor driver input terminals with correct positive & ground polarity.

⚡ Pinout & Connection Summary

Component Arduino Pin Function
Left IR Sensor (OUT) Digital Pin D2 Detects line under left side of chassis
Right IR Sensor (OUT) Digital Pin D3 Detects line under right side of chassis
Left BO Motor (M1) Motor M1 (Pins D9 & D8) Drives left rear wheel
Right BO Motor (M2) Motor M2 (Pins D10 & D12) Drives right rear wheel

⚙️ IR Sensor Calibration Tutorial

  1. Place your Line Follower Robot over the edge of the black electrical tape track.
  2. Rotate the small blue potentiometer screw on the IR sensor module using a mini screwdriver.
  3. Adjust until the LED indicator on the IR module reliably toggles ON when over black tape and OFF when over white surface.
  4. Test both Left and Right IR sensors to ensure equal sensitivity!
